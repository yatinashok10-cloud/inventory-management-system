Privacy policy

Last updated 10 November 2026.

What we store

The items you add (names, SKUs, categories, quantities and prices) are saved in your own browser using local storage. They are not sent to our servers and we cannot see them.

Cookies and analytics

We save one small setting in your browser to remember your answer to the cookie banner. Analytics only runs if you click "Allow analytics". If you click "No thanks", nothing is loaded.

Hosting

Our hosting provider may keep normal server logs, such as your IP address and the pages you opened, for security and to keep the site running.

Deleting your data

Delete items one by one on the main page, or clear this site's data in your browser settings. Because the data lives in your browser, clearing it removes it completely.

Children

Stockroom is not aimed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact
